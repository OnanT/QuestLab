# Nevis Interactive Map Games: Detailed Design & Coordinates

## 🇰🇳 Project Goal
To create 3 distinct interactive Leaflet map games focused on the island of Nevis (Saint Kitts and Nevis Federation), providing an engaging way for students to learn about their local geography and heritage.

---

## 🏗 Game 1: Nevis Heritage Trail
**Objective**: Locate key historical and cultural sites on the island.
**Map Mode**: `leaflet` (Leaflet Map)

*   **Center**: 17.133, -62.610
*   **Zoom**: 13
*   **Tolerance**: 5 (km)
*   **Locations (7)**:
    1.  **Alexander Hamilton House** (17.135, -62.622) - "The birthplace of the first U.S. Secretary of the Treasury, located in Charlestown."
    2.  **Jewish Cemetery** (17.133, -62.621) - "One of the oldest Jewish cemeteries in the Caribbean, found on Government Road."
    3.  **Bath Hotel & Hot Springs** (17.128, -62.617) - "Built in 1778, this was the first hotel in the Caribbean, famous for its volcanic spring water."
    4.  **St. Paul's Anglican Church** (17.132, -62.621) - "A beautiful historic church located in the heart of Charlestown."
    5.  **Fort Charles** (17.126, -62.624) - "A 17th-century fortification south of Charlestown that protected the island from invaders."
    6.  **Cottle Church** (17.177, -62.597) - "The first church in the Caribbean where slaves and masters could worship together."
    7.  **Nelson's Spring** (17.163, -62.620) - "Where Admiral Horatio Nelson took on fresh water for his fleet."
